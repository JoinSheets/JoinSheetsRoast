Congratulations on completing the course — we hope you found it interesting
and genuinely useful! We'd be grateful if you gave this course a star on
GitHub.

By this point you've worked through the full list of DevTools capabilities
(Sources, Console, Elements, Network, Overrides, Coverage, Performance,
conditional breakpoints, DOM breakpoints, Memory) and traced the application's
entire data path — from loading `<script>` tags (chapter 1) to the single most
CPU-heavy computation in the project, ported to another language and compiled
to WASM (chapter 10).

Where you go from here is up to you: you could dig deeper into any of the
topics covered, start reading through other projects like it on your own, with
the understanding of the mechanics you've now built up, or join the JoinSheets
development team and get involved in developing the project further.
