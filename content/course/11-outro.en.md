Congratulations on completing the course — we hope it turned out to be
interesting and genuinely useful to you! We'd be grateful if you starred this
course on GitHub.

By this point you've gone through the full list of DevTools capabilities
covered (Sources, Console, Elements, Network, Overrides, Coverage,
Performance, conditional breakpoints, DOM breakpoints, Memory) and walked the
entire data path of the application — from loading `<script>` tags
(chapter 1) to the single most CPU-heavy computation in the project, ported
to another language and compiled to WASM (chapter 10).

Where you go from here is up to you: you could dig deeper into any of the
topics covered, start reviewing other similar projects on your own with the
understanding you've now built up, or join the JoinSheets development team
and get involved in the project itself.
